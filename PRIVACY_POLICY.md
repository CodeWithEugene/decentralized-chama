# Privacy Policy for DChama

**Last Updated:** 27 January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DChama ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our web-based decentralized application (dApp).

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

As a decentralized platform, we are designed to minimize the collection of personal data. We **do not** collect or store traditional personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or physical address on our central servers. Your interaction with the platform is primarily through your blockchain wallet address.

## 2. The Privacy Layer: Wallet-to-Identity Mapping

DChama utilizes a **Privacy Layer** managed by the KRNL Token Authority to link your blockchain wallet address to your real name. This is essential for the social and trust-based nature of Rotating Savings and Credit Associations (ROSCAs).

- **Visibility is Restricted:** The mapping of your wallet address to your real name is **strictly confidential** and is only revealed to other members within your specific DChama group.
- **No Public Exposure:** Your real name is not stored on a public blockchain ledger. It is held in a private, access-controlled layer accessible only on a need-to-know basis for your group's functioning.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

The link between your wallet and your name is used exclusively for the following purposes:
- To allow DChama group members to identify each other and verify that the correct participants are part of the savings cycle.
- To maintain the integrity and transparency of the group's "Merry-Go-Round" rotation.

We will not share, sell, or disclose this private mapping to any third party outside of your DChama group.

## 4. Blockchain Transactions

All financial transactions (contributions and payouts) are recorded on the Hedera blockchain. These transactions are, by nature, public and auditable. However, they are tied to your pseudonymous wallet address, not your real name.

## 5. Security

We have implemented robust technical measures, including the KRNL-Only Access Control and the Checks-Effects-Interactions pattern, to protect the privacy and security of the platform and its users.

## 6. Your Rights

As we do not hold your private keys, you are in full control of your wallet and the assets within it. You have the right to leave a DChama group according to the rules set forth in the smart contract.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the methods listed on our platform.
