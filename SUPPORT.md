## 🐛 Reporting Bugs

If you encounter a bug or unexpected behavior:

1.  **Search existing issues** on our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/eugene-gabriel/decentralized-chama/issues) page to see if it has already been reported.
2.  If not, **open a new issue** and provide:
    - A clear title and description.
    - Steps to reproduce the issue.
    - Expected vs. actual behavior.
    - Screenshots or error logs (e.g., from the browser console).
    - Your environment details (Browser, Wallet version, Network).

## 💡 Feature Requests

Have an idea to make DChama better?

- Open a **Discussion** or an **Issue** tagged `enhancement`.
- Describe the feature and the problem it solves.
